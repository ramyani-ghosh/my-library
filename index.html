Bring your books home

My Library shelves every book on your Goodreads read shelf, by candlelight.

  1. Open Goodreads → My Books → Import and export.
  2. Click Export Library, then download the file it gives you.
  3. Drop that goodreads_library_export.csv file below.

Your file never leaves your browser.
